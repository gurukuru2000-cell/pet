Our Story

Family Owned Since 2022

Golden Paws Pet Shop was born out of a deep love for animals. Established in 2022 in Colombo, we started as a small family venture with a simple goal: to provide high-quality pet care products that are both accessible and affordable for every pet parent in Sri Lanka.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted destination for pet owners, offering a wide range of premium food, creative toys, and essential grooming products. Our commitment to your pets' well-being drives everything we do.

Our Mission

To provide premium pet care products at affordable prices while ensuring the highest standards of customer service and pet welfare.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and beloved pet care brand in Sri Lanka, fostering a community where every pet is treated like family.
